# KYC

Customers finish KYC from the dashboard before they can invest. The journey can include PAN, DigiLocker when it is required, address, personal details, nominee, bank verification, signature, and review.

Overall status is one of: not started, in progress, a phase checkpoint, submitted (under review), or completed. Step statuses show which part is still pending or failed.

Use only the live KYC status in the customer context. If a step failed, tell the customer to reopen Complete KYC and retry that step. Do not invent application ids, document numbers, or review outcomes that are not in the context.
